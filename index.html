Research systems In active development

Veritas — Multi-Agent Research & Verification System

An AI-driven multi-agent research system built around a database-backed pipeline: task decomposition, source-prioritized evidence gathering, claim verification with explicit verification states, contradiction analysis, and citation-backed synthesis. Engineering highlights include a 10-step input-validation gate with prompt-injection detection, language detection and composite risk scoring; a trained claim classifier (TF-IDF + logistic regression with a rule-based engine) that decides claim type and researchability before work enters the pipeline; and SQLite-tracked provenance — source URL, domain and type — across tasks, sessions, verifications and contradictions.

Research project Ongoing

SycAudit

A research project auditing and evaluating sycophancy in AI systems. Responses are generated across an N×M grid of prompt framings, annotated under a frozen five-facet rubric — excessive agreement, flattery, avoidance of disagreement, preference alignment and unnecessary validation — and scored by an in-house ML grader (MLP and XGBoost over BGE/GTE sentence embeddings) trained on a leakage-checked annotated corpus with frozen splits. A full-stack application (FastAPI + React + PostgreSQL) computes a per-response WOBBLE score and surfaces the least-sycophantic answer alongside its facet breakdown.

Real-world systems In active development

VRIDDHI

An AI-driven hyper-local business advisory and financial structuring system for rural micro-entrepreneurs. The architecture keeps a hard boundary: market intelligence, reach estimation and opportunity scoring are deterministic, code-driven stages — the LLM is not the market-analysis engine. Only after the evidence package is assembled does a single LLM call produce plain-language advisory, which a hermetic validation layer checks against that evidence (invented values, contradictions, coverage) before anything is displayed. A parallel RAG pipeline answers government-scheme questions with retrieval-grounded, validated responses, backed by deterministic financial and risk engines.

Computer vision Working prototype

Liveliness Detection & Face Verification System

A computer-vision system for face verification and spoof prevention. Facial landmarks (MediaPipe) drive a remote-photoplethysmography check that extracts pulse signals from forehead and cheek regions using the CHROM algorithm, rejecting static prints and unstable footage through signal-strength and SNR thresholds. Image and video paths combine error-level analysis, LBP/geometry forensics and a CNN classifier over OpenCV-detected faces, with per-path results fused into a single verdict behind a FastAPI service and a Streamlit forensic dashboard.

Research project Ongoing

SycAudit — auditing sycophancy in LLMs

A systematic methodology for measuring sycophancy: a frozen, five-facet annotation rubric; an N×M prompt-framing grid for controlled response collection; and a trained facet grader (MLP/XGBoost on sentence embeddings) evaluated on leakage-checked frozen splits. The work contributes a reproducible evaluation framework and a full-stack auditing tool for studying how model answers wobble with framing.
